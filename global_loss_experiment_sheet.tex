\noindent\textbf{Problem (baseline, atom-only MAE).}
Per-atom MAE is small (point val.\ 0.003--0.004~eV; planar val.\ 0.014~eV) but net formation energy can fail: $R_{\mathrm{tot}}=100\,|\sum_i(\widehat{\dPE}_i-\dPE_i)|/|\dEtrue|$ with $\dEtrue=\sum_i\dPE_i$.
Signed shifts cancel ($\sum_i|\dPE_i|\gg|\dEtrue|$): median amplification $A=\sum_i|\dPE_i|/|\dEtrue|=8.8$ for planar $|\dEtrue|<0.05$~eV ($n=796$); typical $A\approx 4$--10 overall.
Example Ag--Be (planar, $N=18$): MAE $=0.0046$~eV yet $R_{\mathrm{tot}}=101\%$ because $\widehat{\dE}\approx 0$ while $\dEtrue=-0.036$~eV.
Delivery baseline medians: point $R_{\mathrm{tot}}\approx 20$--28\%; planar val.\ CGCNN $45.8\%$ / Transformer $50.6\%$ ($n_{\mathrm{val}}=198$).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Idea: $\lambda$-weighted global Huber loss (v2).}
Keep per-atom MAE; add a graph-level term on scaled relative net error.
Per batch, graph $g$ with $N_g$ atoms, training mean/std $(\mu,\sigma)$, denorm.\ $\widehat{\dPE}_{g,i}=\sigma\hat z_{g,i}+\mu$:
\begin{align*}
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{atom}}
  &= \frac{1}{|B|}\sum_g \frac{1}{N_g}\sum_{i\in g}\bigl|\hat z_{g,i}-\tilde y_{g,i}\bigr|,
  \qquad
  \dEtrue_g=\sum_{i\in g}\dPE_{g,i},\;
  \widehat{\Delta E}_g=\sum_{i\in g}\widehat{\dPE}_{g,i},\\
  s_g &= \max(|\dEtrue_g|,\,0.05~\mathrm{eV}),\quad
  r_g=\frac{\widehat{\Delta E}_g-\dEtrue_g}{s_g},\quad
  H(r)=
  \begin{cases}
    \tfrac12 r^2 & |r|\le 1\\
    |r|-\tfrac12 & |r|>1
  \end{cases}\\
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tot,raw}}
  &= \frac{\sum_g w_g H(r_g)}{\sum_g w_g+\varepsilon},
  \qquad
  w_g=
  \begin{cases}
    0 & |\dEtrue_g|>50~\mathrm{eV}\ \mathrm{or\ full/graph\ mismatch}>5~\mathrm{eV}\\
    1 & \mathrm{else}
  \end{cases}\\
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tot}}
  &= \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tot,raw}}\,
    \frac{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{atom}}}{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tot,raw}}+\varepsilon}\Big|_{\mathrm{stop\text{-}grad}},\qquad
  \boxed{\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{atom}}+\lambda\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tot}}}.
\end{align*}
Checkpoint global runs on val.\ median $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$; atom-only on val.\ MAE.
Recommended $\lambda$: CGCNN point $0.01$ / planar $0.005$; Transformer point $0.02$ / planar $0.01$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Takeaway.}
Cancellation makes $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ a different objective from MAE; v2 global Huber loss fixes this in controlled sweeps (${\sim}1$--10~pp on CGCNN, up to ${\sim}11$~pp on Transformer), often at ${\sim}0.002$~eV/atom MAE cost on planar.
On delivery CSVs: point Transformer and planar CGCNN improve on val.\ $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ ($26.0\%\to 21.3\%$, $45.8\%\to 32.8\%$); point CGCNN regresses ($28.0\%\to 34.8\%$) despite similar val.\ MAE.
