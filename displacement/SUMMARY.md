# Defect relaxation model: current approach (status 24.11.2026)

Condensed summary of where we stand. The full experiment log is in `RESULTS.md`; figures are in `plots/report/` (`python displacement/plot_report.py`).

## Task

Given an unrelaxed structure with defects, predict:
- **positions:** the displacement vector of every atom, from the unrelaxed structure to the relaxed one (GRACE-2L-UEA-OMAT-large);
- **energy:** the relaxation energy ΔE;
- **cell:** for periodic fault cells, the change of the c vector.

One model handles all three kinds of cell, with no domain switch: point defects, planar faults, and cells containing both.

## Data

| set | source | size |
|---|---|---|
| point defects (C15, 3000-atom cells: antisites on 8a / 16d) | materials team (LAMMPS + GRACE) | 651 cells, 217 compounds |
| planar faults (Laves stacking sequences) | materials team | 1978 cells, ~200 compounds |
| mixed: antisite inside a planar-fault cell | generated by us (ASE + the same GRACE potential) | 1536 cells, 193 compounds |
| new point defects, team recipe (gen_v1) | generated by us, **running** | 593 new compounds, 2965 cells (1779 usable now) |

How we split and handle the data:
- **Compound split:** all splits are by compound, so a test compound is never seen in training for any cell type.
- **Exact energy labels:** point energy labels can come from the exact LAMMPS totals (`data/superset_point_exact.pt`, `--e-exact`). This removed rounding noise but did not change model quality.
- **Validated generator:** on the team's own compounds, our pipeline reproduces their labels to 0.04 meV (median) and 0.2% in displacement (fig 6). It does differ where their minimiser stopped on a symmetric saddle point (5 of 120 pilot cells, 8a antisites). Generation now adds a 0.02 Å random nudge and keeps the lower energy of 2 seeds.

## Model

**Inputs** (one GRACE single point on the unrelaxed structure, plus geometry). The graph is a k3 shell graph within 8.9 Å of the point defect, plus a 4 Å buffer; fault cells use the full periodic cell. Per atom:
- element and initial per-atom energy;
- 3-/4-cycle counts;
- initial GRACE force: its magnitude and its projections on the bonds;
- separate channels for "point defect" (flag, distance to it) and "fault layer" (flag, distance to it).

**Architecture:** equivariant PaiNN (512 hidden, 15 M parameters). The vector output starts from the initial force. A per-atom energy head comes on top, plus a cell head.
- **Recipe:** L1 loss, lr 1e-4, EMA, AMSGrad, LayerNorm, standardised inputs, 250 epochs.
- **Training data:** point + planar + mixed cells together.

**Energy = harmonic estimate + learned residual.**
- The harmonic estimate is ΔE ≈ −½ Σ F₀·d_pred, the energy implied by the predicted displacements and the initial forces.
- The energy head only learns the correction on top of it.
- Position and energy are trained jointly, with energy weight λ = 0.3.

**Production ensemble** (`production/`, `predict_disp.py`):
- **Members:** 2 PaiNN as above (positions + energy) and 2 DefiNet with our inputs (positions only).
- **Combination:** displacements are averaged over all 4; energy is averaged over the 2 PaiNN heads.
- **Cost:** about 2.4 s per cell end to end, almost all of it graph construction on CPU. The model forward is about 15 ms on GPU.

## Results

**Held-out 10% of compounds, production ensemble** (figs 3–5). Metrics:
- r_disp = |error| / |true displacement| per structure, median; 1 = no better than predicting no motion.
- Point defects are scored within 8.9 Å.

| | point | planar | mixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| r_disp (median per structure) | **0.065** | **0.110** | **0.157** |
| pooled skill (1 − Σ\|err\|² / Σ\|d\|²) | 0.90 | 0.58 | 0.39 |
| direction cosine (median, \|d\| > 0.01 Å) | 0.999 | 1.000 | 0.998 |
| relaxation energy, median relative error | 4.2% (vs exact full-cell ΔE) | 5.6% | 4.9% |

**Cross-validated, single models** (3 compound splits × 2 seeds, test compounds unseen; fig 1):

| model | r_disp point / planar / mixed | energy error point / planar / mixed |
|---|---|---|
| unified multi-task + harmonic residual (**used**) | 0.139 / 0.143 / 0.218 | **6.0% / 9.7% / 7.1%** |
| unified multi-task, plain energy head | 0.131 / 0.138 / 0.220 | 7.2% / 11.5% / 9.1% |
| separate position-only model | 0.125 / 0.143 / 0.215 | – |
| separate energy-only model (+ harmonic term) | – | 6.7% / 11.2% / 8.6% |

**Against the published models** (fig 2):
- **On our data**, test r_disp for point / planar:
  - DefiNet as published: 0.30 / 0.28;
  - E3Relax: 0.27 / 0.29;
  - E3Relax-H2: 0.27 / 0.29;
  - ours, single model: 0.14 / 0.14;
  - ours, ensemble: 0.13 / 0.12.
  - About 2× lower error. Our input features also improve DefiNet itself (0.16 / 0.15).
- **On their benchmarks, with their splits:**
  - ours (PaiNN + GRACE inputs) is best or tied on MP, JARVIS and vdW;
  - ours is behind on DefiNet-high, XMnO and C2DB, where relaxations and cell changes are large. Iterative position updates win there.

## Decisions and why

- **One unified model, not separate position and energy models.**
  - Energy: a separate energy model is worse; learning positions helps the energy.
  - Positions: a separate position model is better only on point defects (−0.014 r_disp), and the DefiNet members of the ensemble already recover that.
  - Runtime: a second model would add +79% forward time, but only +0.6% end to end.
- **Harmonic-residual energy.**
  - Energy: better on all three domains (−1.2 / −1.8 / −2.0 percentage points).
  - Cost: a small point-position cost (+0.008), absorbed by the ensemble.
- **Mixed cells in training.** Without them, cells with both defects fail: r_disp 0.39 zero-shot vs 0.22 trained. Point + planar data alone does not cover an antisite inside a relaxing fault.
- **Unified PaiNN replaces the deployed CGCNN / GT energy models in accuracy.** Same residual target (Σ(pe1 − pe0) = ΔE), same test structures, unseen compounds: ΔE error 6.9% / 9.7% (point / planar) vs CGCNN 17.7% / 26.4% and GT 20.7% / 29.7% (fig 7, `energy_compare/`).
- **Exact point energy labels: not adopted for production.** Retraining on them changed nothing (paired, within noise).

## Known limitations

- **Point energy is a view-local quantity.** The model sees ~13 Å around the defect. The far-field part of the full-cell ΔE (p90 ≈ 25 meV) is invisible to it. Against full-cell energies the error is about 7% cross-validated (5% held-out). We earlier reported 5.8% / 3.1%, measured against view sums.
- **Single-compound failures:**
  - Np-Fe planar faults (held out): energy is 4–20× underpredicted and r_disp ≈ 0.5–0.7. It looks like a bulk-like instability. Np-Fe also has several nearby minima in the point data.
  - Large relaxations in general (XMnO / C2DB-like) are where PaiNN loses to iterative models.
- **Label ambiguity:**
  - Some 8a antisites relax off-centre in symmetry-equivalent directions, and no deterministic model can pick the direction.
  - About 4% of the team's 8a-antisite labels are saddle points (fig 6).
- **Cell prediction on mixed cells** is barely better than predicting no change (c MAE 0.047 vs 0.055 Å).
