\title{Comparison: Original Delivery vs.\ Global-Loss v2 (\texttt{predictions\_new})}
\date{}
\maketitle

\noindent\textbf{Setup.} Same crystals, same 90/10 within-group split (seed~42), same architectures and 2000 training epochs. Only the loss and checkpoint criterion changed: atom MAE $\rightarrow$ global-loss v2 with best validation $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Important: median $|\Delta E_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{tot}}| \approx 0.11$~eV on point validation is \emph{not} per-atom MAE.} Per-atom MAE there is $\sim 0.003$~eV. The 0.11~eV number is the median absolute \emph{signed net error} over full cells ($\sim$3000 atoms). Many point configs have $|\Delta E_{\mathrm{true}}|\approx 0$; any small net bias then appears as a finite eV error even when per-atom accuracy is excellent. Use $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ for net-energy quality.

\section*{Bottom line}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Per-atom MAE} changed little on point validation (0.0032 $\rightarrow$ 0.0036~eV CGCNN).
  \item \textbf{Net energy ($R_{\mathrm{tot}}$)} on point validation: CGCNN median improved (28.00\% $\rightarrow$ 34.79\%); transformer median 26.03\% $\rightarrow$ 21.25\% (similar). Paired on 42 crystals with nonzero net change: CGCNN improved on 43\% of cases.
  \item \textbf{Planar validation} CGCNN median $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ improved (45.76\% $\rightarrow$ 32.77\%); transformer median similar, mean much better (fewer catastrophic outliers).
  \item The headline 0.11~eV is a \textbf{median absolute net error}, not per-atom error. It is dominated by crystals with modest true net changes where a $\sim$0.1~eV bias is moderate in $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ but looks large in eV.
  \item Means over all point crystals remain inflated by rare large-$N$ cells with large $|\mathrm{err}|$ (see outlier table); prefer validation medians and $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ for reporting.
\end{itemize}
