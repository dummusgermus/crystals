\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Problem.} Per-atom MAE $\sim$0.005--0.01~eV looks excellent, but total system error can reach $\sim$100\% of the true net energy change when local relaxations cancel. Training optimises per-atom $\Delta\mathrm{PE}$; nothing enforces $\sum_i\widehat{\Delta\mathrm{PE}}_i=\Delta E_{\mathrm{true}}$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Metrics.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathrm{MAE}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_i|\mathrm{PE}_{\mathrm{pred},i}-\mathrm{PE}_{\mathrm{true},i}|$
  \item $R_{\mathrm{tot}}=100\,|\sum_i(\mathrm{PE}_{\mathrm{pred},i}-\mathrm{PE}_{\mathrm{true},i})|\;/\;|\Delta E_{\mathrm{true}}|$
  \item Cancellation $C=1-|\Delta E_{\mathrm{true}}|/\sum_i|\Delta\mathrm{PE}_i|$
  \item Amplification $A=\sum_i|\Delta\mathrm{PE}_i|/|\Delta E_{\mathrm{true}}|$
  \item Blind baseline ($\widehat{\Delta\mathrm{PE}}=0$): $R_{\mathrm{tot}}=100\%$; $\mathrm{MAE}_{\mathrm{blind}}=\text{mean}_i|\Delta\mathrm{PE}_i|$
\end{itemize}

\section*{Bottom-line}

\begin{itemize}
  \item We have a very low per-atom MAE, but not necessarily a low total system MAE. 
  \item $R_{\mathrm{tot}}\approx 100\%$ with MAE $\sim$0.005~eV happens when $\Delta E_{\mathrm{true}}$ is small and per-atom changes cancel ($C\sim 60$--80\%, amplification $A\sim 4$--10$\times$).
  \item Point: median $R_{\mathrm{tot}}\sim 20$--25\%. Planar validation: median $\sim 46$--51\%, mean inflated by small-$|\Delta E|$ outliers.
\end{itemize}
